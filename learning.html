Questions, answered

Before you create your first story

Can a story actually teach something, not just entertain?

Yes. Tell it the word, value or idea you want your child to learn, and Little Story Book Maker builds a short story around it, so learning happens inside a story they want to hear.

Is my child's information safe?

Yes. You are the account holder. Little Story Book Maker is built for parents to create for their children, with no child-facing sign-up or public profile.

What age range is this for?

Little Story Book Maker is designed for ages 2 to 10, and grows with your child as their interests and vocabulary change.

Can I cancel Monthly or Annual anytime?

Yes. Monthly and Annual are straightforward subscriptions with no lock-in. If you'd rather never think about it again, Lifetime is a single payment with nothing to cancel.

What's the difference between the plans?

All three plans let you create unlimited personalised stories. Monthly and Annual are recurring access at different commitment lengths; Lifetime is one payment for permanent access.
