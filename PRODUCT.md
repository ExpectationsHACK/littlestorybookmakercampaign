# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. No build step, no framework — matches every other page already in this project.

## Users

Nigerian parents (predominantly mothers), ages 25–45, with children ages 2 to 10. Time-pressed; want meaningful bedtime/reading moments with their kids but don't always have the time or energy to find or read the "right" book. Reached primarily via Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad campaigns, landing on one angle-specific page before deciding to subscribe. Most traffic and reading happens on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Little Story Book Maker lets a parent create a short, personalised children's story in minutes, built around what their child loves, is learning, or a value the parent wants to teach. It exists to turn reading time into real bonding time, and to give parents a fast way to create a story that feels made specifically for their child rather than searching for a generic one.

## Positioning

Personalisation is the mechanism a shelf of books can't copy: a story built in minutes around this specific child's name, interests, and the exact word or value the parent wants to introduce today.

## Operating Context

Parents create stories through the web app at littlestorybookmaker.com. This project (littlestorybookmakercampaign) is a set of standalone ad-landing pages, each built around one campaign angle (speed/convenience, personalisation, learning, bonding, "next favourite," "hero of their story"), funding traffic into the main site via CTA buttons.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Parent tells the app what the story should be about (a value, a word, a favourite thing); the app generates a short personalised story in minutes.
- Three plans: Monthly ₦2,500/month, Annual ₦24,000/year, Lifetime ₦38,500 once. Confirmed — do not alter without explicit instruction.
- No real product screenshots or app-usage footage exist yet. Do not fabricate app UI or "in-app" imagery presented as real; illustrative/decorative visuals are fine if not presented as literal screenshots.
- No payment integration is wired yet; CTAs point to littlestorybookmaker.com.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: "Little Story Book Maker" (also seen styled "Little Storybook Maker" in source copy — either form is acceptable). Domain: littlestorybookmaker.com. No other binding visual identity for this specific page — the user has explicitly authorised replacing the current navy/cream/storybook visual world here.

## Evidence on Hand

Real marketing copy exists for the bonding angle: "There will be a last time your child asks you to tell them a story," the four-part "what goes into a bonding story" (what they love, what they're learning, a value to plant, a few minutes fully together), and Monthly/Annual pricing with a genuine 20% annual discount. No customer testimonials, logos, press mentions, or real product photography exist — none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Personalisation is the whole pitch — make it concrete (a value, a word, a specific child), never abstract.
2. Considered purchase for a parent, not an impulse buy — warm and trustworthy tone, not hard-sell.
3. Real urgency only — no fabricated scarcity or countdowns.
4. Mobile-first — most traffic and reading happens on a phone.
5. No fabricated evidence — no fake testimonials, screenshots, or stats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement beyond standard web accessibility (semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, color contrast), already practiced across the existing pages.
